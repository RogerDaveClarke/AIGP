# AIGP Certification Journey

### Study plan, research, practical learning, and exam preparation
This repository documents my journey toward the IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) certification. It brings together my study timeline, milestones, tasks, chapter summaries, additional research, and exam preparation.

My goal is to deepen my ability to translate responsible AI principles into practical decisions across product development, technical program management, and the AI lifecycle.
## Why I am pursuing AIGP
My experience spans AI products, services, enterprise platforms, and cross-functional technical programs. Through this certification journey, I have lead compliance (Privacy, Trust, Security and Legal) for enterprise services in Microsoft I want to pivot my career in that direction, strengthen how I assess AI risks, establish accountability, and build governance into the way AI systems are designed, evaluated, deployed, and monitored.

I will also explore how these concepts apply to [**Demcratic AI**](https://github.com/RogerDaveClarke/DemocraticAI.AI), my project focused on parliamentary transparency and responsible AI. These reflections will connect study material to concrete questions about bias, evidence, transparency, human oversight, and public trust.

## How I will study each chapter
Each chapter will follow a consistent learning cycle:

1. **Read:** Identify the chapter's central questions and concepts.
2. **Summarize:** Explain the material in my own words.
3. **Reflect:** Capture new learnings, challenged assumptions, and unresolved questions.
4. **Apply:** Connect concepts to a realistic AI governance scenario.
5. **Research:** Investigate gaps and record supporting sources.
6. **Review:** Use recall questions and revisit areas that remain unclear.

Chapter notes will include:

- Chapter title, reading date, and source edition.
- A concise summary and key terminology.
- My principal learnings and reflections.
- Practical implications for products, programs, and governance.
- Relevant exam objectives.
- Additional reading and open questions.
- A short self-assessment and next review date.

## Research and source quality
Research notes will distinguish **source material**, **my interpretation**, and **questions still to be validated**. References will include the source, publication or version date where available, access date, and why the material matters.

For legal, regulatory, standards, and exam-related topics, I will prioritize authoritative sources and check that the material applies to the relevant jurisdiction and version. Older notes may remain useful as learning history but should not be treated as current guidance.

## Exam preparation
Exam preparation will focus on understanding and applying concepts, supported by:

- Mapping my study coverage to the applicable official exam outline.
- Building a glossary and concise revision notes.
- Creating original recall questions and scenario-based exercises.
- Recording practice results and the reasoning behind incorrect answers.
- Revisiting weak areas through targeted reading and spaced review.
- Completing a final readiness and exam logistics checklist.

Practice performance will guide further study; it will not be treated as a guarantee of exam readiness or success.

## Responsible use of AI
If I use AI to support study, I will use it to help explain concepts, challenge my reasoning, organize notes, or generate original practice scenarios. I remain responsible for checking accuracy, validating references, and demonstrating my own understanding.

Material AI-assisted contributions will be identified where relevant. Confidential information and protected exam content will not be included.
