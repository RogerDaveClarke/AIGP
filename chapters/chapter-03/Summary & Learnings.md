## Summary
### Ecosystem: Typical actors
- Standards bodies who provide guidance on how to implement AI governance
- Research institutes publish technical and policy research advancing responsible and human centric AI. This research comes from universities, governments and non-profits
- Vendors supply platforms and services to support AI governance
- Assurance providers who audit AI systems to valid they are operating as expected and comply with legal regulations
- Professional associations provide opportunties to learn, grown and join AI practioneers communities
- Customers procure and use AI systems with an increasing demand for transparency in how they are governed


### AI Learning and Development
Organizations are approach this based on three core themes:
- Foundational understand of AI
- Advanced knowledge (risk mitigation, compliance or ethics)
- Domain knowledge is an understanding of specific functions

**Recommendations**
- Integrate governance into employee onboarding and company training programs
- Use multiple formats with real-world examples
- Integrate critical thinking skills
- Use a central repositor, curate and refresh content
- Utilise metrics to determine engagement
  
There are 8 recognized functions with AI governance, akin to the European Cyber Security Skills Framework.
- Policy: Aligns organizational policies with the AI policy landscape, creates internal/external policies for development, deployment and use of AI systems and training and awareness for these. Skills: research and analyse laws, regulations and industry standards, draft policies, engage and align stakeholders. Key knowledge areas are: Organizational strategy for AI and its governance, AI development life cycle, principles and concepts, relevant laws, regulations and industry standard. Skills are program/project management, tool and vendor evaluation, data tracking and analysis, training and development. 
- Governance: Create framework policies and controls. Tasks: Build/procure tools to run the program, track and report metrics and risks, monitor compliance and promote literacy and upskilling
- Technical: Develop, deploy and maintain responsible AI systems. **Tasks:** build/test AI systems, document results and mitigations, monitor/maintain production systems, contribute to AI policy development. **Skills:** AI System development, testing and eval, Preparation and analysis of technical documentation, RCA and translate technical capabilities/limitations into governance and risk insights.
- Product: Integrate AI capabilities into a product. **Tasks:** define strategy, translate governance requirements, prioritize AI capabilities, prepare user facing documentation, monitor and maintain products/services. **Skills:** Product Strategy/Life cycle management/roadmap, UI design, comms with technical and non-technical audiences
- Ethics: Oversight of AI systems. **Tasks:** Establish and embed and operationalize RAI across an org including dev life cycle and ethical awareness. **Skills:** Translated ethical principles into practices, communication and training for technical/non-technical audiences. 
- Risk: 
- Legal
- Assurance
