# Chapter 13: Test Questions - Post-Deployment Monitoring and Maintenance

> Practice questions in the style of the AIGP certification exam (single-best-answer multiple choice, 4 options). Try to answer all questions before checking the answer key at the bottom.

## Questions

1. What is a key reason post-deployment monitoring is necessary even after thorough pre-deployment testing?
   A. AI system behavior and performance can drift or degrade over time as real-world data and conditions change
   B. Pre-deployment testing guarantees permanent accuracy
   C. Monitoring is only needed for safety-critical systems
   D. Monitoring has no relationship to model performance

2. What does a "confusion matrix" help measure in AI system monitoring?
   A. Network latency
   B. Classification performance via true/false positives and negatives across groups
   C. Data storage costs
   D. Legal compliance status

3. What is "LLM-as-a-judge" used for in monitoring generative AI systems?
   A. Making final legal rulings on AI-related litigation
   B. Using an LLM to automatically evaluate the quality or correctness of another model's outputs
   C. Replacing all human review entirely and permanently
   D. Training new models from scratch

4. Which ISO/IEC standard specifically addresses bias in AI systems and AI-aided decision-making?
   A. ISO/IEC 42001
   B. ISO/IEC 24027
   C. ISO/IEC 22989
   D. ISO/IEC 15288

5. What is a commonly cited challenge in post-deployment monitoring of AI systems?
   A. Metrics are always identical across all AI use cases
   B. Defining meaningful, context-specific metrics and thresholds for qualitative or emergent risks (e.g., large-scale societal impacts)
   C. Monitoring tools are universally standardized with no gaps
   D. There is no need to monitor human factors

6. Under the EU AI Act, what obligation applies to high-risk AI system providers regarding monitoring once the system is on the market?
   A. No post-market obligations exist
   B. Post-market monitoring, including systems for detecting and reporting serious incidents
   C. Monitoring is solely the deployer's responsibility, never the provider's
   D. Monitoring obligations expire after 30 days

7. Why might an organization use "LLM tracing" tools in production environments?
   A. To track and debug the sequence of model calls, prompts, and outputs for troubleshooting and auditability
   B. To replace model evaluation entirely
   C. To automatically rewrite model weights
   D. Tracing has no monitoring value

8. What is an example of an "emerging trend" in AI monitoring discussed in governance literature?
   A. Elimination of all monitoring requirements
   B. Increased use of automated observability and evaluation tooling integrated into cloud AI platforms
   C. A permanent moratorium on monitoring generative AI
   D. Monitoring is being phased out industry-wide

9. Why do organizations typically treat security-focused monitoring as separate from "general" AI performance monitoring?
   A. Security monitoring is not a real governance concern
   B. Security monitoring addresses a distinct risk domain (e.g., attacks, misuse) often requiring specialized tools and expertise
   C. Performance monitoring already fully covers security
   D. Security is irrelevant to deployed AI systems

10. What is a key purpose of defining monitoring metrics during the design phase rather than only after deployment?
    A. Metrics defined late are always more accurate
    B. Early definition ensures the right data/instrumentation is built into the system to support meaningful post-deployment monitoring
    C. Design-phase metrics have no bearing on post-deployment monitoring
    D. It eliminates the need for any dashboards

## Answers

1. A
2. B
3. B
4. B
5. B
6. B
7. A
8. B
9. B
10. B
