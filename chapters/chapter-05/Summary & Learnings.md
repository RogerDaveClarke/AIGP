## Summary
There is no single AI governance model for regulating AI. Many countries have adoption a soft non-binding and voluntary approach, often sector or technology specific and have use a distributed model leveraging existing legislation and regulatory bodies. Countries like Brazil and the U.K are moving closer to hard horizontal binding legislation, but most take a more cooperative approach with industry. All models share common principles such as safety, transparency but the implementation of these principles differs greatly. To stay abreast of changes, you can do the following:

1. Track legal developments using the IAPP Global AI Law and Policy Tracker
2. Understand the underlying technology
3. Prioritize key juristictions
4. Identify common and diverging requirements to build your own governance program

## Key Terminology
- Hard law: Provides legal certainty and enforceability using a risk-based approach with stringent requirements. It consists of legally binding legislation, regulations and judicial decisions and can be horizontal applying across sectors or vertical applying to specific areas.
  - Legislation: sets for forth obligations an organization must follow
  - Regulations: provide details on how legislation should be implemented
  - Judicial decisions: apply remedies for failure to comply and help interpret the application of legalisation and regulation  
- Soft law: does not create binding legal obligations, whose adoption is voluntary and fills gaps in hard law or where hard law does not exist. It fosters share views among governments, industry and technical experts providing flexible easy easily updatable instruments. Soft can be categorized into Ethical Guidelines such as the OECD AI Principles, Industry and Technical Standards such as the NSIT AI RMF and Government-issued frameworks such as the Singapore model.
- Hybrid: A combination of hard law and soft law elements offering flexibility and supporting innovation. The hard law elements set clear enforceable boundaries and the soft law provides guidelines and standards.
