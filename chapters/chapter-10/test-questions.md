# Chapter 10: Test Questions - Best Practices for Training AI

> Practice questions in the style of the AIGP certification exam (single-best-answer multiple choice, 4 options). Try to answer all questions before checking the answer key at the bottom.

## Questions

1. What governance risk is most directly associated with using large volumes of unvetted scraped web data for model training?
   A. Guaranteed improved model accuracy
   B. Potential inclusion of copyrighted, biased, or personal data without appropriate rights or safeguards
   C. Elimination of all bias
   D. No risk; scraped data is always public domain

2. What is "synthetic data" in the context of AI training?
   A. Data collected directly from real individuals without consent
   B. Artificially generated data designed to resemble real-world data, often used to supplement or substitute real datasets
   C. Data that has been fully anonymized real data
   D. A type of hardware accelerator

3. Why is a documented "data strategy" considered a governance best practice before training a model?
   A. It has no impact on data quality or compliance
   B. It helps ensure data sourcing, rights, quality, and governance considerations are addressed deliberately rather than ad hoc
   C. It is only relevant for small datasets
   D. It replaces the need for a privacy impact assessment

4. What does "shaping model behavior" (e.g., via reinforcement learning from human feedback) primarily aim to achieve?
   A. Increasing model size indefinitely
   B. Aligning model outputs with desired behaviors, values, and safety constraints
   C. Removing all training data lineage
   D. Bypassing evaluation requirements

5. Which practice helps address data provenance and licensing risk during data preparation?
   A. Ignoring data source terms of use
   B. Tracking data lineage and verifying licensing/terms of use (e.g., robots.txt, Creative Commons terms) before use
   C. Deleting all metadata immediately
   D. Using only data with no attached terms

6. What is "deduplication" in the context of training data preparation, and why does it matter?
   A. It is irrelevant to model quality
   B. Removing duplicate records, which helps reduce overrepresentation bias and training inefficiencies
   C. A legal requirement unrelated to data quality
   D. A technique exclusive to structured databases

7. Why might an organization choose to use a clickwrap agreement when licensing third-party data for AI training?
   A. Clickwrap agreements have no legal effect
   B. To establish documented, enforceable terms governing permitted use of the data
   C. To avoid any data governance obligations
   D. Because it is required for all open data

8. What risk does "data pipeline" governance primarily aim to manage across the training lifecycle?
   A. Risks related to ongoing data collection, transformation, and quality as pipelines evolve, not just a one-time dataset snapshot
   B. Only risks occurring after model deployment
   C. Only risks related to hardware failure
   D. Risks are static and do not need ongoing governance

9. Why is the structured vs. unstructured data distinction relevant to AI training governance?
   A. It has no bearing on processing or risk approaches
   B. Different data types may require different preparation, quality, and governance approaches (e.g., labeling, validation)
   C. Only unstructured data can be used for AI training
   D. Only structured data poses privacy risk

10. What is a key consideration when managing the training process regarding reproducibility and documentation?
    A. Avoiding any record of training configuration
    B. Capturing training configurations, data versions, and parameters to support auditability and troubleshooting
    C. Relying solely on verbal communication among engineers
    D. Reproducibility is not a governance concern

## Answers

1. B
2. B
3. B
4. B
5. B
6. B
7. B
8. A
9. B
10. B
