# Chapter 9: Test Questions - Best Practices for Designing AI

> Practice questions in the style of the AIGP certification exam (single-best-answer multiple choice, 4 options). Try to answer all questions before checking the answer key at the bottom.

## Questions

1. Why is the design phase often described as a high-leverage point for AI governance?
   A. Because decisions made early are cheaper and easier to change than those made after deployment
   B. Because design-phase issues cannot be fixed later anyway
   C. Because governance has no role before deployment
   D. Because the design phase has no bearing on downstream risk

2. What is the primary purpose of an "AI system proposal" early in development?
   A. To finalize marketing materials
   B. To document the intended use case, objectives, risks, and governance requirements before significant investment
   C. To bypass legal review
   D. To replace the need for testing

3. Which of the following best describes an AI "control framework" in the design phase?
   A. A set of physical security locks for servers
   B. A structured set of technical, procedural, and organizational safeguards mapped to identified risks
   C. A marketing plan for AI products
   D. A pricing model for AI services

4. What does "AI system analysis" during design typically involve?
   A. Ignoring intended use cases
   B. Assessing data sources, model choices, intended users, and potential risks/impacts of the proposed system
   C. Only evaluating the system after deployment
   D. Focusing exclusively on cost estimates

5. Why is a documented "development readiness" review important before moving an AI system toward deployment?
   A. It is a purely ceremonial step with no governance value
   B. It confirms that risk assessments, controls, and approvals are in place before further investment or release
   C. It replaces the need for post-deployment monitoring
   D. It applies only to third-party vendor systems

6. Which of these best reflects the principle of "privacy by design" as applied to AI system development?
   A. Addressing privacy only after a system is fully built and deployed
   B. Embedding privacy protections into the system architecture and processes from the earliest design stages
   C. Avoiding any privacy assessment altogether
   D. Relying solely on end-user consent banners

7. What is a key reason to define intended use (and misuse) cases during AI system design?
   A. Misuse cases are irrelevant to governance
   B. Clearly scoping intended and foreseeable misuse helps tailor appropriate controls and risk assessments
   C. It eliminates the need for any technical testing
   D. It only matters for safety-critical systems

8. Which stakeholder group should typically be involved in reviewing an AI system proposal for governance purposes?
   A. Only the engineering team that built the model
   B. Cross-functional reviewers such as legal, privacy, security, and business risk stakeholders
   C. Only external auditors
   D. Only the end customer

9. What is a common output of the "AI system analysis" stage that supports later governance activities (e.g., audits)?
   A. A finalized marketing brochure
   B. Documentation of system purpose, data, risks, and design decisions (e.g., supporting a model card)
   C. A list of unrelated competitor products
   D. An employee time-tracking report

10. Why might "development readiness" criteria include sign-off from a governance committee or designated approver?
    A. To ensure accountability and confirm risk mitigations are adequate before proceeding
    B. Because no other approval mechanism exists in any organization
    C. Because this step is required only for open-source models
    D. Because it eliminates all future monitoring obligations

## Answers

1. A
2. B
3. B
4. B
5. B
6. B
7. B
8. B
9. B
10. A
