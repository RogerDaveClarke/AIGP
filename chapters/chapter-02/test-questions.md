# Chapter 2: Test Questions - How to Develop an AI Governance Program

> Practice questions in the style of the AIGP certification exam (single-best-answer multiple choice, 4 options). Try to answer all questions before checking the answer key at the bottom.

## Questions

1. What is the primary purpose of an AI governance program within an organization?
   A. To eliminate all AI-related risk
   B. To establish policies, processes, and accountability structures that ensure AI is developed and used responsibly
   C. To replace the organization's legal department
   D. To automate all compliance reporting

2. Which international standard provides a framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system within an organization?
   A. ISO/IEC 27001
   B. ISO/IEC 42001
   C. ISO 9001
   D. ISO/IEC 22989

3. What is "shadow AI"?
   A. AI systems formally approved by an organization's governance committee
   B. The unsanctioned or unmonitored use of AI tools by employees outside of approved channels
   C. An AI model trained exclusively on synthetic data
   D. A backup AI system used for disaster recovery

4. Which of the following is considered a best practice when building an AI governance framework?
   A. Limiting governance participation to the IT department only
   B. Aligning AI governance with existing enterprise risk management and compliance structures
   C. Deferring all governance decisions until after deployment
   D. Avoiding documentation to preserve flexibility

5. An AI "hallucination" is best mitigated at the governance-program level by which practice?
   A. Ignoring low-confidence outputs
   B. Establishing human review, grounding, and output validation processes
   C. Removing all guardrails to increase model creativity
   D. Disabling model logging

6. Why might an organization adopt a cross-functional AI governance structure instead of placing governance solely within one department?
   A. It is legally mandated in every jurisdiction
   B. AI risks span legal, technical, ethical, and business domains that no single function can address alone
   C. It reduces the need for executive sponsorship
   D. Cross-functional structures are cheaper to operate

7. Which of these is a primary driver for organizations to formalize AI governance programs?
   A. Decreasing use of AI across industries
   B. Growing regulatory scrutiny and reputational/operational risk from ungoverned AI use
   C. Elimination of all data privacy laws
   D. Lack of AI adoption generally

8. What distinguishes an "AI governance framework" from an "AI governance program"?
   A. They are identical and interchangeable in all contexts
   B. A framework is the structural/methodological approach (policies, roles, controls), while a program is the organization's ongoing operational implementation of that framework
   C. A framework only applies to generative AI
   D. A program cannot include a framework

9. Which of the following best reflects a mature AI governance practice regarding AI inventories?
   A. Maintaining a central inventory of AI systems, their purpose, risk level, and owners
   B. Avoiding documentation of AI systems to reduce legal discovery risk
   C. Tracking only systems that use generative AI
   D. Limiting inventories to systems built in-house

10. Why is executive/board-level sponsorship considered important for an AI governance program?
    A. It has no material effect on program success
    B. It signals organizational priority and ensures adequate resourcing and accountability
    C. It is only relevant for publicly traded companies
    D. It replaces the need for a dedicated AI governance team

## Answers

1. B
2. B
3. B
4. B
5. B
6. B
7. B
8. B
9. A
10. B
